Help with gameplay, learning, and privacy

Get help

Email eksenworks@gmail.com and include your device model, operating-system version, LingoClue version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or exported learning data.

Progress and backups

Learning progress and custom decks are stored locally. Use the in-app export option before changing devices or deleting the App. Eksen Works cannot recover data that was not exported.

Privacy

Read the LingoClue Privacy Policy for details about local data, advertising, and consent.
