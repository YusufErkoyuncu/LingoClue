Eksen Works · LingoClue

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with the LingoClue mobile application (the “App”), including Android and iOS versions that link to this policy. No account is required. Eksen Works does not operate an application backend for LingoClue and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment-card information.

Information stored on your device

LingoClue stores learning progress, spaced-repetition history, settings, local profiles, statistics, and custom decks on your device. This information provides the App’s learning and gameplay features. You can delete local progress inside the App or clear or remove the App’s local data using your device settings.

Import and export

If you choose to import or export a backup, your device’s document picker lets you select the source or destination. Eksen Works does not receive the selected file. Any storage provider or destination app you choose handles the file under its own privacy practices.

App-store purchases

LingoClue may offer an optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase through the store from which you obtained the App, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. LingoClue does not receive your payment-card or bank details. The App uses purchase information supplied by the store, such as product, purchase status, and transaction or purchase token, to recognize and restore the entitlement. A local ownership setting may be retained on your device for offline use.

Advertising

Advertising is deferred and disabled in the current Android release. The App does not request or display ads when no production advertising configuration is present. If advertising is enabled in a future Android or iOS release, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before distribution.

Text-to-speech and device features

The App can use platform text-to-speech and supported device sensors for optional features. Text-to-speech is handled by the speech engine installed or selected on your device; that provider’s privacy practices apply. Eksen Works does not receive speech text or sensor readings through an Eksen Works server.

Service providers and network access

Core learning features work offline. When you use store purchases, Google or Apple may process device, account, transaction, network, diagnostic, and security information under their policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. Eksen Works does not sell personal information.

Children’s privacy

LingoClue does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal contact information. Parents or guardians can clear the App’s local data through device settings and may contact Eksen Works using the developer contact shown on LingoClue’s store listing.

Security and retention

Learning data remains in the App’s private local storage until you delete it, clear application storage, or uninstall the App, subject to platform and device behavior. Information processed by an app store is retained and protected according to that provider’s policies and account controls. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can use the core App without making a purchase. You can reset local progress inside the App, clear application storage, choose whether to import or export files, and manage purchases through the applicable app store.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LingoClue, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact shown on LingoClue’s Google Play or Apple App Store listing. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information in a support request.
