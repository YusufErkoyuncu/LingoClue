Eksen Works · LingoClue

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with the LingoClue Android application (the “App”). No account is required. Eksen Works does not operate an application backend for LingoClue and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment-card information.

Information stored on your device

LingoClue stores learning progress, spaced-repetition history, settings, local profiles, statistics, and custom decks on your device. This information provides the App’s learning and gameplay features. Android application backup is disabled. You can delete local progress inside the App or clear the App’s storage in Android settings.

Import and export

If you choose to import or export a backup, Android’s document picker lets you select the source or destination. Eksen Works does not receive the selected file. Any storage provider or destination app you choose handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Google Play purchases

LingoClue offers an optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the transaction under Google’s terms and privacy policy. LingoClue does not receive your payment-card or bank details. The App uses purchase information supplied by Google Play, such as product, purchase status, and purchase token, to recognize, acknowledge, and restore the entitlement. A local ownership setting may be retained on your device for offline use.

Advertising in this release

Advertising is deferred and disabled in the current release. The App does not initialize Google Mobile Ads and does not request or display ads when no production advertising configuration is present. If advertising is enabled in a future release, this policy and the App’s disclosures will be updated before distribution.

Text-to-speech and device features

The App can use Android text-to-speech and supported device sensors for optional features. Text-to-speech is handled by the speech engine installed or selected on your device; that provider’s privacy practices apply. Eksen Works does not receive speech text or sensor readings through an Eksen Works server.

Service providers and network access

Core learning features work offline. Network permissions are present for Google Play Billing and for advertising functionality that is disabled in this release. When you use Google Play Billing, Google may process device, account, transaction, network, diagnostic, and security information under the Google Privacy Policy. Eksen Works does not sell personal information.

Children’s privacy

LingoClue does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal contact information. Parents or guardians can clear the App’s local data through Android settings and may contact Eksen Works using the developer contact shown on LingoClue’s Google Play Store listing.

Security and retention

Learning data remains in the App’s private local storage until you delete it, clear application storage, or uninstall the App. Information processed by Google Play is retained and protected according to Google’s policies and account controls. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can use the core App without making a purchase. You can reset local progress inside the App, clear application storage, choose whether to import or export files, and manage Google Play purchases through Google Play.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LingoClue, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact shown on LingoClue’s Google Play Store listing. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information in a support request.
