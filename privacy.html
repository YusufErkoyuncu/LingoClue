Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Eksen Works handles information in connection with the LingoClue mobile application (the “App”), including Android and iOS versions that link to this policy. No account is required. Eksen Works does not operate an application backend for LingoClue and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment-card information.

Information stored on your device

LingoClue stores learning progress, spaced-repetition history, settings, local profiles, statistics, and custom decks on your device. This information provides the App’s learning and gameplay features. You can reset local progress inside the App or remove the App’s local data using your device settings.

Import and export

If you choose to import or export a backup, your device’s document picker lets you select the source or destination. Eksen Works does not receive the selected file. Any storage provider or destination app you choose handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Advertising and consent

The iOS version uses Google Mobile Ads to show native and interstitial advertisements. Before requesting ads, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to provide privacy options where applicable. Depending on your consent choices, device settings, and region, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and consent information to deliver, limit, measure, and protect ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ad availability can vary by platform, region, consent choice, inventory, and application configuration. The App’s core gameplay and learning features remain available when an ad cannot be requested or shown.

Text-to-speech and device features

The App can use platform text-to-speech and supported device features. Text-to-speech is handled by the speech engine installed or selected on your device; that provider’s privacy practices apply. Eksen Works does not receive speech text or sensor readings through an Eksen Works server.

Children’s privacy

LingoClue does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal contact information. Parents or guardians can clear the App’s local data through device settings and may contact Eksen Works through the support page below.

Your choices and retention

You can reset local progress, remove local application data, choose whether to import or export files, and use the in-app privacy-options control when Google requires it. Local information remains until you delete it, clear application storage, or uninstall the App, subject to platform behavior. Service providers retain information under their own policies and controls.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LingoClue, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the LingoClue Support page or email eksenworks@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information in a support request.
